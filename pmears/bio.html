About the Author

Dr. Paul K. Mears

Command Sergeant Major. SWAT Commander. Federal Air Marshal. Professor. Author. More than thirty years spent on the front line of security, and now writing its history.

His Book on Amazon Book a Talk

Service

Dr. Paul Mears is a military and law enforcement professional with more than 30 years of service. He served in the U.S. Army as a Command Sergeant Major (Special Operations / Communications) and as a U.S. Coast Guard Drug Interdiction Boat Commander.

Law Enforcement

After his military career he joined law enforcement in Florida, rising through the ranks from officer to Captain. He led Death Row operations, managed high-risk transports, and served on SWAT as a Sniper and Commander, as well as Emergency Response Commander for ten years.

He later worked as a Federal Air Marshal, Criminal Investigator, and Field Supervisor before retiring in 2013. He is an NRA-certified firearms instructor and holds a 6th-degree black belt in Isshinryu Karate and Aikido.

International Work

From 2014 to 2018 he trained police and special forces in Abu Dhabi and Dubai, where he served as a Professor of Global Security Studies and received recognition from royal leadership.

Today

Dr. Mears works in executive protection and security operations in the United States. He holds a Ph.D. in Criminology / Counterterrorism along with multiple degrees in criminal justice and investigations, and maintains several Florida security and investigation licenses.

“I spent a career responding to threats. Writing about them is how I make sense of where they came from.”— Dr. Paul K. Mears
