How the paw works
  • The paw never does the task. It only shows what could go wrong.
  • Literal reads your words at their narrowest and most inconvenient. Presumptuous fills every gap with a confident wrong guess.
  • Each curse names the exact words (or the missing detail) that cause it, how bad it is, and the smallest fix.
  • Apply the fixes your own way and wish again — or surrender to see the fixed prompt (free) and copy it.
  • When nothing meaningful is left, the paw concedes — and a concession doesn't cost a wish.
  • Three wishes per paw. Every curse describes something real AI models do — no magic.
