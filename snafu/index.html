SNAFU

Trap your rivals. Bite their tails. Don't hit the wall.
Player 1  WASD, diagonals QEZC or two keys together
Player 2  arrow keys (two together for diagonals), or numpad 1–9
With one human, both sets control Player 1. Gamepads work too (pad 1 = P1, pad 2 = P2).
Touch  drag the on-screen disc.  Space start / continue  P/Esc pause  M mute

Trap: trails never stop growing. Crash into a wall, an obstacle or any trail and you're out. Everyone still alive scores a point each time someone crashes. No 180° turns.
Bite: two serpents of 10 segments that grow toward 20 if left alone. Run your head into the other serpent's tail to bite off a segment. Hitting walls, obstacles, their body or your own costs you a segment. First to zero loses the round.
